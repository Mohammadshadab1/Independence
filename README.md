# 🇮🇳 Azadi.2026 — 80 Years of Independence

An interactive, single-page tribute website celebrating **80 years of India's Independence**. Built to walk visitors through the freedom struggle — from the first uprising in 1857 to the midnight of 15 August 1947 — through a clean, tricolour-inspired design.

**🔗 Live Website:** [Independence Day](https://independence-self.vercel.app/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Timeline** — Six defining moments of the freedom movement (1857 → 1947)
- **Meet the Heroes** — Profile cards for 10 freedom fighters with expandable bios
- **What the Tricolour Means** — Saffron, White & the Ashoka Chakra, and Green explained
- **National Symbols** — Anthem, national song, emblem, flower, bird, and animal
- **Wish Wall** — Visitors can pin a short wish for India (session-based)
- **The Flag Code** — Quick rules for flying the national flag with dignity
- **Bilingual Support** — Toggle the entire site between **English** and **Hindi**
- **Fully Responsive** — Optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- **Scroll Progress Bar** — Tricolour-themed progress indicator as you scroll

## 🛠️ Built With

- HTML5, CSS3 (no frameworks)
- Vanilla JavaScript
- Google Fonts (Fraunces, Rajdhani, Newsreader)
